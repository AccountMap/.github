# AccountMap

**See how your accounts recover each other, before you get locked out.**

AccountMap maps every account you have to the email, phone number, device, or authenticator that gets you back in, then shows the weak spots: single points of failure, SMS-only recovery, and accounts with no way back. It's local-first, encrypted on your device, and open source.
